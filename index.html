Cerberus1

for V.

Sullen Hades wanders these tenebrous warrens,
Bereft of sun and hope,
Awaiting the return of Persephone.

I, too, pine for our beloved queen.

When autumn summons her descent,
At her feet I will rest my several heads
(Upon her hem,
The scent of earth above!)
And as she scratches behind my many ears—
Though the world turn barren and cold,
Though Demeter weep—
I will contentedly
Thump my serpent tail.

1 Author’s note: Written from the perspective of a decidedly chimeric pooch (first curr-son?!), this turgid verse is necessarily and tautologically doggerel. ↩
